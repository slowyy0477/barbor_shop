Ayan Beauty Salon - Easy Android Installation

1. On the computer, find:
   E:\Barbar-Shop\Ayan-Beauty-Salon-v1.0.0.apk

2. Send this APK to your Android phone with a USB cable or a private file
   transfer. Do not upload it to a public website.

3. On the phone, open Files, tap the APK, allow Files to install unknown
   apps when Android asks, then tap Install.

4. Open the app. A fresh install has no demo customers or demo money records.
   The installed name is the neutral "Salon" until step 10 is done.
   If the shop laptop is switched on with the salon server running, the app
   finds that server by itself. Nothing has to be typed in.

5. Customers sign in with their Pakistani mobile number and their own 4 to 6
   digit PIN. A new customer creates the PIN while making the profile. Five
   wrong attempts pause sign-in for 15 minutes.

6. Customers see Home, Book, Wallet and More. They do not see an Owner menu.
   For this offline single-phone pilot, the owner taps the AB salon mark five
   times quickly and enters the initial code 530146. Change that code in
   Owner Settings immediately.

7. Owner > Customers > edit a customer > Reset sign-in PIN clears a forgotten
   PIN. That customer sets a new PIN at the next sign-in.

8. Haircut photos: the owner uploads photos under Owner > Services & staff >
   Add haircut style. Customers see them animate in the Haircut styles strip
   and can tap a photo to open the full look.

9. To share one set of customers, bookings and wallet between several phones,
   run the laptop server (START-HERE.txt, Part 2). The phone finds the published
   address automatically; if a phone still says "Offline app on this phone",
   paste the laptop's https address into Owner > Settings > Salon server
   address and save. Customers can also simply open the permanent link
   https://slowyy0477.github.io/barbor_shop/ in any browser.

10. Owner > Settings > "Phone app name & logo" saves your salon name and logo
    on this phone. The launch screen shows them from the next start, and
    Android offers a home-screen icon with the same name and logo. Android
    cannot rename an app once it is installed, so keep that icon on staff
    phones.

Important:
- Without the laptop server, this APK is an offline single-device pilot. Its
  data is not shared between phones and it is not the source of truth for real
  money.
- The shared version keeps customer, booking and wallet data on the laptop
  server. Real SMS codes need one provider account (ops\connect-sms.cmd);
  card payments and payouts still need provider accounts later.
- Keep the release signing key private. It is required for future updates.
