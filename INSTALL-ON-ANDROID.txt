Salon App - Easy Android Installation

1. On the computer, find:
   E:\Barbar-Shop\Salon-App-v1.0.0.apk

2. Send this APK to your Android phone with a USB cable or a private file
   transfer. Do not upload it to a public website.

3. On the phone, open Files, tap the APK, allow Files to install unknown
   apps when Android asks, then tap Install.

4. Open the app. A fresh install has no demo customers or demo money records.
   The installed name is the neutral "Salon" until step 10 is done.
   The app is offline: every customer, booking and wallet entry stays on this
   phone, and nothing is sent to the internet.

5. Customers sign up and sign in with their Pakistani mobile number and their
   own password of 10 to 20 characters (at least one letter and one number).
   There is no SMS code anywhere in the app. Five wrong attempts pause sign-in
   for 15 minutes.

6. Customers see Home, Book, Wallet and More. They do not see an Owner menu.
   The owner taps the salon mark (the round logo at the top of the screen) five
   times quickly, then enters the owner access code.
   The first code is 530146. Change it in Owner Settings immediately, because
   the app was built with that code and anyone who reads these files knows it.

7. Owner > Customers > edit a customer > "Set new password" fixes a forgotten
   password, because no code can be sent to recover it. Tell the customer the
   new password; they can change it themselves from More after signing in.

8. Haircut photos: the owner uploads photos under Owner > Services & staff >
   Add haircut style. Customers see them animate in the Haircut styles strip
   and can tap a photo to open the full look.

9. Each phone keeps its own customers, bookings and wallet. To use the same app
   on an iPhone instead, open https://slowyy0477.github.io/barbar_shop/ in
   Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

10. Because the data stays on the phone, run Owner > Settings > "Export
    workspace data" every week and keep that file somewhere safe. If a phone is
    lost, reset or the app is uninstalled, its data goes with it.

11. Owner > Settings > "Phone app name & logo" saves your salon name and logo
    on this phone. The launch screen shows them from the next start, and
    Android offers a home-screen icon with the same name and logo. Android
    cannot rename an app once it is installed, so keep that icon on staff
    phones.
