Salon App - Start Here

This app is OFFLINE. Everything stays on the phone it is installed on.
Nothing runs on any computer, and nothing is sent to the internet.


HOW TO GET THE APP ON A PHONE

Android

1. Copy E:\Barbar-Shop\Salon-App-v1.0.0.apk to the phone (USB cable or your own
   cloud storage).
2. Tap the file on the phone.
3. Android says the file is from an unknown source. Tap Settings, allow
   "Install unknown apps" for the app you are using, then tap Install and Open.

iPhone

1. Open https://slowyy0477.github.io/barbor_shop/ in Safari.
2. Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
3. It opens like a normal app from your home screen.

Both are the same app with the same data model. Pick one per phone.


CUSTOMER SIGN IN

- New customer: tap Sign in, then Create new account. Type the name, the mobile
  number and a password twice, tick the promotions box, then tap Create account.
  The customer is signed in straight away.
- The password must be 10 to 20 characters and must have at least one letter and
  at least one number. Example: SalonPass2026.
- Coming back later: the same mobile number and the same password. The phone
  stays signed in, so they normally do not type it again.
- Five wrong password tries pause sign in for 15 minutes.
- Forgot the password? Only the owner can set a new one:
  Owner > Customers > Reset password.


OWNER MENU (customers never see it)

1. Tap the round salon logo at the top of the app 5 times quickly.
2. Type the owner access code and tap Sign in.
   The first code is 530146. Change it on your first day, because it is the code
   the app was built with.
3. Tap Lock when you finish.


SET THIS UP ONCE

1. Owner > Settings
   - Salon name, tagline, phone, address, opening hours.
   - Phone app name and logo (shows on the launch screen and the home screen
     icon).
   - Owner access code (change it from 530146).
2. Owner > Services & staff
   - Your services with prices and durations, and your barbers.
   - Haircut styles: add a photo, name, price and description. Customers see
     them on the home screen with a "Book this look" button.
3. Owner > Settings
   - Wallet top-up amount, first deposit bonus, expiry days, reminder cycle and
     referral reward.
   - Every amount is in PKR and can be changed at any time. Changing a price
     never changes an old bill.


WHAT THE APP DOES BY ITSELF

- A completed visit updates the customer's history and works out the next
  suggested visit date.
- A wallet payment is split into paid credit and bonus credit. The bonus is a
  one-time new customer benefit and is never given twice.
- A confirmed booking holds the slot, and the customer can book again in one tap
  from a reminder.
- A referral reward stays Pending until the new customer completes a paid visit.
- Old bills and wallet entries are never deleted. A mistake is fixed with a
  reversal entry, and every manual adjustment needs a reason.


KEEP A COPY OF YOUR DATA

Everything lives on that one phone. If the phone is lost, reset or uninstalled,
the data goes with it.

- Owner > Settings > Export workspace data writes a copy you can keep.
- Do that at the end of each week and keep the file somewhere safe.


KEEP THESE PRIVATE

- The owner access code.
- Customer names, mobile numbers and wallet history.
- The release keystore and android\release-signing.properties on the computer.
  The same key is needed for every future update of the app, so back them up.
